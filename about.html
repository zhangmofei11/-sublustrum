About Sublustrum.wiki

Sublustrum.wiki is an unofficial fan-made guide for Sublustrum, the upcoming psychological adventure game developed by Brickworks Games in partnership with Phantomery Interactive.

This site provides comprehensive coverage including story walkthroughs, puzzle solutions, character profiles, and in-depth lore exploration of the mysterious Flickering World.

Our goal is to help players prepare for the game's Fall 2026 release and understand the rich psychological and atmospheric experience that awaits.

About the Game

Sublustrum is a psychological adventure game about a writer searching for his missing scientist brother. Following cryptic clues, he discovers a machine that immerses him into the "Flickering World"—a surreal landscape where memories become places and reality dissolves.

Originally released internationally as "Outcry" in 2008, the game won Best Adventure Game and Best Sound Design at the KRI Awards. The 2026 reimagining brings this cult classic to modern platforms with fully explorable 3D environments.

Key features include sound-driven puzzles, atmospheric horror inspired by David Lynch and Tarkovsky's Stalker, and a steampunk-meets-Victorian aesthetic.
