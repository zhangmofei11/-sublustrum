STORY WALKTHROUGH

Navigate the Flickering World and find your brother. Contains story spoilers.

I

THE AWAKENING

Prologue — The Real World

You receive a cryptic letter from your brother, the scientist, inviting you to his laboratory. Upon arrival, you find the lab abandoned with strange machinery humming in the basement.

Key Observations:

  • • Examine the brother's desk for research notes
  • • Find the photograph of the Sublustrum device
  • • Discover the journal entries about "consciousness exploration"

The machine awaits. Once you enter, there is no turning back.

II

THE FLICKERING WORLD

Chapter 1 — First Contact

You awaken in a surreal landscape where reality bends. This is the Flickering World—a manifestation of the subconscious mind. Your brother's presence is everywhere, but nowhere.

Objectives:

  • • Learn basic interaction with the environment
  • • Find the first memory fragment (the childhood home)
  • • Locate the Frequency Device

Puzzle Hint:

The house door won't open normally. Listen for the humming sound from within—the pitch reveals the combination.

III

ECHOES OF MEMORY

Chapter 2 — The Industrial District

The industrial sector represents the brother's scientific mind—factories of thought, machinery of logic. Here you'll face audio puzzles that test your hearing.

Objectives:

  • • Navigate the steam-powered corridors
  • • Solve the harmonic resonance puzzle
  • • Find the "Father's Workshop" memory

Puzzle Hint:

Three pipes, three tones. Match the sequence from the brother's childhood song (humming it helps).

IV

THE VOID BETWEEN

Chapter 3 — The Dark Sanctuary

The deepest layer of the subconscious. Here, the brother's greatest fears manifest. Shadowy figures pursue you through endless corridors.

Objectives:

  • • Escape the Shadow presence
  • • Recover the "Last Conversation" memory
  • • Reach the Central Chamber

Warning:

The Shadows cannot be fought—only evaded. Use silence and shadow.

V

THE TRUTH

Chapter 4 — Confrontation

You've found your brother, but he is not who you remember. The machine has changed him—or perhaps revealed who he truly was.

The Choice:

Two endings await, determined by the memories you've collected and the truths you've uncovered.

Ending A: Awakening

Both of you escape the machine. But the Flickering World follows you home.

Ending B: Submersion

You choose to stay in the Flickering World with your brother. Reality fades away.

MEMORY FRAGMENTS
