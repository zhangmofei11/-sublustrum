THE STORY

You are a writer whose scientist brother has vanished. Following cryptic clues, you discover a strange machine that immerses you into the Flickering World—a surreal landscape where memories become places and reality dissolves.

As you explore this liminal space between consciousness and subconscious, you realize the machine was designed to explore the human mind itself. But your brother is somewhere inside, lost in his own creation. Can you find him before the Flickering World consumes you both?

KEY FEATURES

🌀

The Flickering World

A surrealist dreamscape where memories manifest as physical locations. Nothing behaves as expected.

🎵

Sound-Driven Puzzles

Music and audio are central to gameplay. Tune frequencies, follow voices, solve acoustic riddles.

🗝️

Lateral Thinking

Classic adventure puzzles with modern twists. Think beyond conventional solutions.

🎭

Psychological Horror

Atmospheric tension inspired by David Lynch and Tarkovsky's Stalker. Fear of the unknown.

⚙️

Steampunk Aesthetics

Victorian-era machinery meets retrofuturism. Brass, clockwork, and mysterious devices.

💀

Near-Death Themes

Explores consciousness at the edge of life. Dreams, dying, and the space between.

A ATMOSPHERE

Dark Ambient Score

Originally featuring music by Anthesteria, the soundtrack creates an oppressive, dreamlike atmosphere that responds to your actions.

Visual Design

Decadent steampunk aesthetics merged with Victorian mystery. Every frame is a painting, every shadow tells a story.
